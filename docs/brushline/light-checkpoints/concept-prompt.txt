Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE polished production concept board with three equally sized landscape panels stacked vertically, for Brushline Rally's intermediate rally checkpoint redesign. Reference images 1, 2 and 3 show the respective existing game environments and checkpoint placement: autumn sunset ridge, bright coastal road, twilight highland. Use them as environment and camera references. Replace the two inflatable checkpoint posts beside each road with pure vertical LIGHT COLUMNS. Keep roads fully open and unobstructed, no arch or overhead beam. Keep the existing stylized low-poly comic colour blocking, atmospheric painted skies, black outline only on the rally car, no outlines on environment or lights. No car brands or sponsors.
All three use identical minimal hardware: a squat graphite hexagonal projector puck, 0.75 metres wide, 0.22 metres tall, thin luminous ring around the lens. From the lens a 5 metre tall beam rises: a bright narrow white-tinted core, saturated colour surrounding it, soft transparent outer glow and softly fading top; no physical pole, no glass cylinder, no tube, no conical spotlight, no swirling ribbons. Slight vertical streaks and sparse ascending light flecks. A restrained pool of coloured light on the ground around each base. Columns should be visible in daylight without washing out landscape; keep the horizon and turn readable. Pair of columns at road shoulders where existing posts stood.
Panel 1: sunset ridge autumn landscape with GOLD foliage and peach/lavender sky; use ICE CYAN light (#61E8FF).
Panel 2: bright coastal landscape pale cream grass, blue sky; use ORCHID VIOLET light (#D69AFF) for contrast with the blue-green coastal palette.
Panel 3: deep INDIGO twilight highlands; use WARM AMBER GOLD light (#FFC46B), gentle enough to preserve nighttime view.
Composition: rear driving perspective in each panel, car at bottom foreground, checkpoint near centre midground prominent and clearly visible; camera similar to each reference. Include tasteful small caption at panel corner exactly "SUNSET RIDGE / ICE CYAN", "SUNLIT CAPE / ORCHID", "MISTY HIGHLANDS / AMBER". This is an implementable game VFX design reference, not a cinematic science fiction portal. No enormous bloom, no lightning, no inflatable structures, no lasers crossing the road.
