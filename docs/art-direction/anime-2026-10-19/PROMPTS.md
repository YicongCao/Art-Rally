## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Produce one highly polished landscape art-direction concept board for the ORIGINAL Godot indie rally game ART RALLY, approximately 16:9. This is a future visual target, not a screenshot of an implemented feature. Reference image is the existing game's screenshot: preserve the original cream-white short FWD hatchback with teal roof/side stripe, compact proportions, orange rear lamps, original number 16 livery and white five-spoke wheels; the recognizable sunset mountain forest setting and clear rally road. Reimagine the art, not the game identity. Inspired by confident Japanese anime cel rendering and bold Japanese RPG graphic design, using original UI layouts and original branding only.
Layout consistent across three alternative direction boards: left 75% is one large convincing gameplay concept view, rear three-quarter chase camera at human driving height, car lower center taking roughly 22% of frame, driving a gravel road bending left through golden autumn hills, red/orange tree crowns, teal conifers, a small red wooden cabin on the left, mountains and clouds behind. Road and braking sightline remain clear. Upper-left small ART RALLY identifier and a simple vector-style left-turn pictogram; upper-right readable timer '01:24.630'; lower-left compact minimap; lower-right strong readable '87' 'KM/H' and gear '3'. HUD uses short labels, vector-style pictograms, clean spacing, never overlaps car or road. No dense text.
Right 25% separated by clean negative space: upper part a high-quality three-quarter front vehicle modeling/material study of the SAME cream/teal number 16 hatchback, demonstrating silhouette, black contours and material tones; lower part a small original title/menu design study with ART RALLY, three short buttons 'RACE', 'PRACTICE', 'SETTINGS', RACE has a persistent vector check and contrasting selected fill. Large confirm/right arrow and smaller back/left arrow treatment where appropriate.
Critical: ONLY THE VEHICLE has BLACK drawn contour outlines and selective black body seam linework. Trees, mountains, rocks, road, cabin and clouds must have ZERO drawn outlines; they are formed by painted color/value shapes. Wheels also cleanly inked. The vehicle should feel like an animated drawing occupying a coherent three-dimensional world. No real brands, no Persona characters, no copied game logo, no generic photorealistic PBR, no global edge-detection filter, no watercolor blur obscuring road, no excessive speed lines, no giant caption. Upper board corner tiny direction identifier and English direction name.

## A — 疾速墨线

Direction A — Graphic anime rally. Bold energetic graphic-novel/cel animation treatment: warm vermilion accents, ink-black and warm ivory UI, teal and amber autumn scenery, strongly separated two-to-three tone shadows, crisp angular typography and asymmetrical cut-paper panels. Use occasional restrained halftone on car shadow areas and menu only. Environment has bold clean painted color masses, absolutely no contour ink. Car slightly more muscular, carefully beveled original hatchback, black outline 2–3 pixels at 1080p and selective interior body seams. Menu angular but readable; high adrenaline, controlled graphic impact.
Board identifier: A.

## B — 夕映动画

Direction B — Cinematic painted anime rally. Luminous hand-painted animation-background art: peach sunset sky, ultramarine/lavender shadows, golden grass, vermilion deciduous crowns, deep teal conifers, softly painted atmospheric mountains. Environment absolutely no ink outlines; deliberate broad gouache-like color groups and subtle organic brush edges, not watercolor blur. Original cream/teal rally hatchback has elegant rounded bevels, fluid roof arc, crisp two-to-three cel shading and fine black silhouette plus restrained black panel seams. UI has clean dark blue-ink/ivory/amber asymmetrical tabs and excellent readability, fewer jagged cuts, confident contemporary Japanese animation design. Warm, vivid, cinematic; not pastel washed out.
Board identifier: B.

## C — 套色拉力

Direction C — Vintage motorsport screenprint anime. Distinct collectible 1980s Japanese rally-poster aesthetics: restricted petrol teal, mustard yellow, burnt orange, warm ivory, plum shadows. Matte posterized color fields, coarse intentional print grain confined to large shaded areas, restrained halftone, screenprinted forms with no environment outlines. Environment no black strokes; distant mountains are elegant simplified layered cut-paper shapes, foliage grouped into block-printed painted masses, warm long shadows. Car uses bold clean black outline and deliberate ink panel seams, strong original geometric retro body silhouette with rounded wheel arches, white five-spoke wheels, cream/teal livery. HUD and menu resemble beautifully art-directed racing magazine print typography with tire-tread accents; controlled and legible, no grunge covering numbers.
Board identifier: C.
